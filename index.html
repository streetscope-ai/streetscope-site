↓ See how it works

Three steps. Under two minutes.

No app install. Works in any mobile browser.

01

Film a 20-second clip

Walk or drive past the front of the property. Keep the facade in frame. That's it — handheld is fine.

02

Upload and wait ~60 seconds

Our local GPU pipeline runs frame extraction, object detection, segmentation, depth, and material classification on your clip.

03

Get a structured condition record

A top-line score plus per-component breakdown — siding, paint, windows, gutters, trim, foundation, driveway, and more — each with confidence level and evidence frames.
